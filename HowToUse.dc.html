Quick fixes

The Google sign-in didn't open.

  • Click the Minute Minder icon in Chrome.
  • If you don't see it, click the puzzle icon and pin Minute Minder.
  • Try signing in again.

I don't see the timer in Google Meet.

  • Refresh the Google Meet tab.
  • Check that Minute Minder is installed and pinned.
  • Make sure you're inside a Google Meet call.
  • Reopen Minute Minder.

Reminders pop up too often.

  • In your dashboard, turn off the reminders you don't need or change when they appear.

I signed in. What now?

  • Create a Google Calendar event with a Google Meet link.
  • Join the call. The timer appears automatically.
